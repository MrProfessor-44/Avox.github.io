Our story

Avox Group is a diversified construction, engineering and real estate development company, established to contribute to national development while giving clients dependable service and long-term value. Today our core focus is land acquisition, development and plot sales.

Mission

To become a leading construction and real estate development group, recognised for quality execution, transparent land solutions and sustainable community development.

Why we exist

Because buying land should feel safe, and owning it should lead somewhere. We create value, enable progress and build foundations for future generations.

What sets us apart

  • Transparent, documented land sales. Ownership process and paperwork are explained, not hidden.
  • Land plus the means to develop it. Feeder roads, bush clearing, building and engineering sit in the same group.
  • Visible credibility. Registered company and professional body membership shown openly.
  • Direct, human contact. Four phone lines, WhatsApp and a local Ndola office.
